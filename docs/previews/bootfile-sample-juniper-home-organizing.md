Juniper Home Organizing

### Business Description

A fictional independent home organizing business helping people create practical systems for everyday spaces.

### Audience

Busy households who want a calmer, easier-to-use kitchen.

### Offer

A kitchen reset session that helps a household sort the space and choose a practical place for everyday items.

### Goal

Invite interested households to ask about a kitchen reset.

### Call To Action

### Constraints

Do not invent a price, discount, duration, testimonial, or promise of a particular result. Use a warm, practical tone.

## Promotional email

**Subject:** Could your kitchen work a little better for you?

**Preview:** Start with the everyday essentials and the storage you already have.

Hello,

If your kitchen feels harder to use than it needs to, we can start with the items you reach for every day.

Juniper Home Organizing offers a kitchen reset session: a chance to sort kitchen items and choose a practical place for everyday essentials, using the storage you already have.

There is no need to aim for a showroom. The starting point is your household and how you use the space. We will discuss pricing and availability before you decide whether to book.

Reply KITCHEN to ask about a kitchen reset.

Juniper Home Organizing
